The Vision

Talent is Universal.
Opportunity is Not.

Growing up in Kigali, Rwanda, I witnessed countless athletes with world-class talent but limited access to the resources, exposure, and networks needed to reach their full potential.

After pursuing my own basketball journey through Belgium, the Netherlands, and Luxembourg—while building a career in tech consulting—I realized I had a unique opportunity to give back.

Rwandaballers was born from a simple belief: every talented athlete deserves a chance to showcase their abilities on a global stage.

BN

Benjamin Ngarambe

Founder & Pro Basketball Player

Rwanda • Belgium • Netherlands • Luxembourg

Global Connections

Bringing international coaches, scouts, and opportunities directly to African talent, bridging continents through basketball.

Elite Training

Professional-level training sessions, physiotherapy, and mentorship from experienced coaches who have competed at the highest levels.

Community Impact

Beyond basketball—building confidence, discipline, and life skills that empower the next generation of Rwandan leaders.

"My dream is to establish a permanent training hub in Rwanda—a space where athletes can train, recover, and learn year-round."
